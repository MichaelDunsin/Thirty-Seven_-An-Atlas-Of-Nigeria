About this atlas

Thirty-Seven is an atlas of Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. All thirty-seven pages are written. Each one lists its sources.

Map data

  • State boundaries: geoBoundaries, from GRID3 Nigeria state boundaries (2022), CC BY 4.0. Simplified for the web.
  • Rivers and lakes: Natural Earth, 1:10m, public domain.
  • Relief: ETOPO global relief model (NOAA), 20 arc-minute grid, interpolated and generalised.

Figures and pictures

  • Nigeria's last census was in 2006. Population figures here are 2022 projections by the National Population Commission and National Bureau of Statistics, as tabulated by City Population.
  • Illustrations and textile patterns are original drawings. The patterns are inspired by textile and craft traditions and are decorative, not reproductions.
